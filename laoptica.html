Questions

Helpful information before you visit.

Everything you need to know for a smooth visit to LA OPTICA EYECARE.

Do I need an appointment for an eye test?

We recommend booking before you visit so we can prepare for your appointment and help minimise waiting time. Use the form above, message us on WhatsApp, or call +91 95625 96660.

What should I bring for my eye test or new glasses?

Please bring your current glasses and any existing prescription if you have one. This helps us understand your current needs and discuss suitable options.

How long do prescription glasses take?

Delivery time depends on your prescription, selected frame and lens type. Our team will confirm an estimated ready date before you place your order.

Can you adjust or repair my frame?

Bring your eyewear to the store and we will inspect it first. We can then explain whether an adjustment or repair is possible.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Please call or message us before your visit for the latest accepted payment options.

Is parking available nearby?

Parking availability can vary during the day. Call us before visiting if you would like local guidance.
